ClearSky

Clear air for distant shots.

v2.1

Scores are heuristics, tuned for New England dew-point and wind habits until you calibrate them in the shot log. A score is a guide to which hours deserve a closer look, not a promise. Forecast confidence comes from lead time, the spread of the forecast models and a cross-check between the NWS and Open-Meteo.

Data: National Weather Service forecast grid for temperature, dew point, humidity, wind, cloud cover, visibility and rain; Open-Meteo for cloud layers, air quality and smoke, the forecast ensemble, the cross-check and ERA5 reanalysis (Copernicus); NOAA SWPC Kp; Zippopotam.us and OpenStreetMap Nominatim for places. Open-Meteo's free tier is for personal, non-commercial use. Only coordinates leave your device; nothing is stored anywhere but this browser.
