ClearSky

Clear air for distant shots.

__VERSION__

Scores are heuristics, tuned for New England dew-point and wind habits until you calibrate them in the shot log. A score is a guide to which hours deserve a closer look, not a promise. Forecast confidence comes from lead time, the spread of the forecast models and the NWS cross-check.

Data: Open-Meteo forecast, ensemble, air quality and ERA5 reanalysis (Copernicus); NOAA SWPC Kp; NWS cross-check; Zippopotam.us and OpenStreetMap Nominatim for places. Free tiers are for personal, non-commercial use. Only coordinates leave your device; nothing is stored anywhere but this browser.
